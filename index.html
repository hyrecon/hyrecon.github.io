About

Hi ! My name is Hanyeol Ryu (You can just call me Hanyeol :D) and I am a research assistant in the Department of Electronics Engineering at Pusan National University (B.S. '26), advised by Prof. Sangkil Kim.

Outside the lab, you'll find me swimming 🏊, running 🏃, making coffee ☕, or exploring electronics 💻 just for the fun of understanding how they work.

I'm curious about the enduring philosophical questions — what is justice, and how ought we to live 👨‍🦳 — as well as the mysteries of the universe 🪐, like what happens to information inside a black hole ⚫. I always welcome a conversation about any of these over coffee.

Research

Wireless Communications

I aim to make wireless connectivity truly sustainable — building ultra-low-power links where the same waves that carry data also sense the environment, converging toward integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) in next-generation networks.

AI / Machine Learning

My goal is to put intelligence inside the radio itself — lightweight deep neural networks that learn channel behavior on-device, enabling wireless systems that adapt in real time without relying on the cloud.

Battery-Free IoT and Smart Infrastructure

I envision a world where connected devices never need charging — backscatter tags and energy-autonomous platforms weaving buildings, roads, and everyday objects into a self-sustaining sensing fabric.

My research focuses on low-power wireless communications (e.g., backscatter communications), wireless sensing, and artificial intelligence to enable next-generation intelligent sensing and communication platforms for IoT applications.
