Research

Wireless Communication for Battery-Free IoT and Smart Infrastructure

I aim to build wireless systems that power the battery-free IoT — ultra-low-power backscatter links where the same waves that carry data also deliver energy and sense the environment, converging toward integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) for self-sustainable smart infrastructure.

AI / ML for Intelligent Wireless Communication

My goal is to put intelligence inside the radio itself — designing AI models for specific applications and tailoring them to the physics, data, and resource constraints of wireless systems, so that on-device intelligence can run in real time without relying on the cloud.

Wireless Security & Privacy

I seek to secure wireless infrastructure across communication and sensing systems — understanding how systems can be spoofed, jammed, or covertly tracked, and building defenses that detect hidden devices through their unique RF emanations while preserving localization privacy.

I am broadly interested in the following areas:
